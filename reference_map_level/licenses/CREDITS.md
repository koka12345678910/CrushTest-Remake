## South zone (2026-11-03)

No new outside assets. `Tools/south_zone.gd` paints the ravine, mire, boardwalks and paths procedurally. Its sprites come from the style sheet above, cut by `Tools/ruin_art.gd`:
- the broken bridge stubs and the fallen pieces, from `bridge_long`;
- the rope bridge, from `jetty`;
- stumps and fallen trees, from the sheet's stumps and dead trees turned by a quarter.

The wisp mote is procedural. The reeds still come from the existing `Map/Tileset/Tileset/watertiles.png` (see above).
